STUDY 2027OFFLINE GUIDE

1. Application dates for 2027 entry

Applications for 2027 opened 1 March 2026 and generally closed 30 September 2026 via CAO.

Dates were checked in November 2026. Universities change deadlines without warning — always confirm on the official website before you rely on a date.

2. Fees and funding

Check the official MUT fee schedule — technology programmes vary by department.

  • Tuition is usually charged per module or course — your final bill depends on the subjects you register for.
  • Residence, meals, books, transport and registration levies are separate from tuition.
  • If your household income is R350,000 or less per year, check NSFAS — it can cover tuition and residence at public universities.
  • Ask the university for a written fee quotation before you register.

3. What to prepare before you apply

  1. A certified copy of your ID or passport.
  2. Your latest school results and subjects (and predicted results if you are still in Grade 12).
  3. Your APS score — most universities publish a calculator on their site.
  4. Proof of address and any supporting documents the portal asks for.
  5. A working email address you check daily — all updates go there.

Complete the application yourself. Many universities will not accept applications submitted by agents.

4. Accommodation

  • Residence applications often close on the same date as, or before, the main application deadline — apply early.
  • Ask whether the residence fee includes meals, and what the deposit is.
  • Check the official residence page for application dates, room types and contact details.
  • Verify the campus location — Umlazi, Durban — before arranging transport or a lease.

5. Student life

  • Join at least one student society or club in your first month — it is the fastest way to find friends and support.
  • Universities of technology are practical and career-focused; expect project work, workplace learning and industry-linked qualifications.
  • Use the campus health service, counselling and academic support early — you do not have to wait until you are struggling.
  • Part-time work and study support are available; ask the student affairs office.
